Fixed Stop Loss

Stop price STAYS fixed. Even if BTC rises to $90k, stop loss stays at $79,977. Profits are not protected.

Trailing Stop Loss

Stop automatically FOLLOWS UP when BTC rises. But if BTC drops, stop STAYS at the highest position.
